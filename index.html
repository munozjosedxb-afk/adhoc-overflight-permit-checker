COUNTRY-LEVEL PERMIT LOGIC: FIRs are used only to identify which countries the route crosses. Multiple FIRs, FIR re-entries, or several FIRs inside the same country produce one country permit decision and one country permit request. OVERFLIGHT (OVF) and TAKEOFF/LANDING (T/O-LDG) are separate permit checks. OVF applies to overflight countries; T/O-LDG appears only for the departure and arrival airport countries. REVIEW means the requirement or validity still needs operational verification; the tool will not invent a permit validity period.
